## Delivery Information
- **Address accuracy:** Shipments use the exact details you provide at ordering; incorrect details cause delays.
- **Timeframes:** Registered deliveries take 1–30 working days depending on location.
- **Customs holds:** Destination customs may delay an order up to 4 weeks for inspection or random security checks. This affects a small percentage of orders and is uncontrollable.

---

## Customs, Duties & Taxes
Strong Pulses International Co. Ltd. is not responsible for any customs or taxes. All such fees (tariffs, taxes, etc.) are the customer's responsibility.

- The recipient is the importer of record and must ensure the product can be legally imported and comply with all destination-country laws.
- The recipient may owe import taxes, duties, and clearance fees once the shipment arrives.
- Customs, duties, and taxes are **non-refundable**. If a shipment is refused due to unexpected import fees, original and return shipping costs are not refunded.
- Customs policies vary by country; contact your local customs office for details. Clearance procedures can cause delays beyond delivery estimates.

## Why DHL Express?
As Taiwan is an island, reliable affordable worldwide shipping is limited. After comparing many carriers, DHL was chosen for speed and service. It is expensive, but we take no profit from shipping. Fees vary by zone — see the rate table and payment page.
