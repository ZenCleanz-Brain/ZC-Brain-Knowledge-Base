# HEALING CRISIS DURING ENZYME CLEANSE: UNDERSTANDING DETOX REACTIONS

## Introduction

What’s a Detox Reaction?
 If you feel unwell during an enzyme cleanse, it’s not a setback, it’s a Healing Crisis. Also known as a Herxheimer Reaction or Die Off Effect, it’s your body’s way of showing that deep detox is in motion and healing has begun.

---

## Why It Happens
 When you take high-activity enzymes:

Your cells activate and detox systems awaken

Toxins like plaque, chemicals, and undigested waste are released

Elimination pathways like colon, kidneys, lungs, skin work overtime

You may feel temporary discomfort as your body clears what doesn’t belong

**Common Detox Reactions
 Digestive**: Bloating, gas, loose stool = intestinal cleansing
 Head & Mood: Headaches, fatigue, mood swings = blood and brain detox
 Skin: Rashes, pimples = toxins exiting through the skin
 Urine: Dark, smelly, frequent = kidneys flushing waste
 Respiratory: Mucus, bad breath = lung and sinus clearing
 Full-body: Fever, chills, aches = immune system recalibrating

How to Support Your Healing Process
 • Drink plenty of warm water to flush waste
 • Prioritize rest and sleep
 • Eat light, clean food like fruits, steamed vegetables, broths (if you are not water fasting)
 • Dry skin brushing before showers or sauna to support lymph flow
 • Use a sauna or take hot baths to encourage toxin release
 • Gentle movement or self-massage to circulate energy
 • Meditation and breathwork to release emotional tension
 • Journaling or sharing circles if you have company to support emotional processing
 • If symptoms are too intense, reduce or pause enzyme dose for 1–2 days

How Long Does It Last?
 Most people feel it for 1 to 3 days. For some, up to a week.
 Rarely symptoms are will last longer.

Who’s Most Likely to Experience a Detox Reaction?
 • People new to cleansing or enzyme detox
 • Those with chronic constipation or sluggish digestion
 • Individuals with high toxic load from meds, alcohol, processed food
 • Low-movement lifestyles or emotional stress
 • Anyone holding onto years of unprocessed waste physically or emotionally

Finally ,...
 A detox reaction is not a side effect.
 It’s your body releasing what’s been holding you back.
 Once the storm passes, clarity returns.
 Let it go. Then let it glow.
