**THE JOURNEY BEHIND ZENCLEANZ** 

**From Healing Arts to Human Sovereignty** 

ZenCleanz was not born from a business idea. It emerged from a lifelong quest to understand why some people regain their vitality while others continue to struggle despite having access to endless health information, therapies, and products. 
For more than four decades, Daniel Li Ox, known as Zuman, immersed himself in Traditional Chinese Medicine, Medical Qi Gong, fasting, detoxification, and natural healing. Through years of clinical practice, teaching, and working with thousands of individuals, he discovered that people were not lacking information—they were overwhelmed by it. 
The world of natural healing had become a maze of conflicting advice and fragmented solutions. What people needed was not another technique, but a coherent framework—a map capable of guiding them back to the body's natural capacity for healing and regeneration. 

**The Lessons of Fasting** 

Long before fasting became popular, Zuman began exploring therapeutic water fasting as a tool for self-healing and renewal. Over the course of his life, he accumulated more than 800 days of water fasting, including numerous extended and month-long fasts. 
These experiences revealed a profound truth: the body possesses extraordinary intelligence. 
When interference is reduced and the right conditions are restored, the body naturally moves toward balance, vitality, and regeneration. 

This led to a principle that would later become central to the ZenCleanz philosophy: 
**The body is rarely failing. More often, it is overwhelmed.**

Unlike conventional supplements, these preparations transform hundreds of plants through years of fermentation into highly bioavailable enzymes, peptides, organic acids, postbiotics, and beneficial metabolites. 
The results were remarkable. 
For the first time, Zuman saw a practical way to make deep detoxification accessible to ordinary people without requiring prolonged fasting or major lifestyle disruption.
