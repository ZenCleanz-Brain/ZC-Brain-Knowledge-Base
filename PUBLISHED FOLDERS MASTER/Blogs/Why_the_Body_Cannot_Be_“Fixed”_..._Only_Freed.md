This belief is one of the greatest sources of suffering on Earth.

The human body is not a simple machine.

It is not a car with replaceable parts or a device with an instruction manual.

It is a living cosmos, a complex, evolving organism shaped by millions of years of adaptation, refinement, and evolutionary genius.

Inside your body, every nano-second, millions of actions, reactions, transmissions, biochemical cascades, electrical currents, enzymatic dialogues, cellular exchanges, detoxification processes, and regenerative impulses unfold with perfect precision.
